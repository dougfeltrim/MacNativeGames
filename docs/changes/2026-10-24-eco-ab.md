## Interpretation and limits

The key disabled the keep-alive during actual game rendering and median present cadence remained 33.33 ms. Eco increased game GPU time, consistent with downclocking; clock frequency was not measured. Short pacing deviations occurred, including one discarded present in an earlier Eco 600-present window; last windows had zero discards. Eco sampling was marked unclean by the inherited runner due to long-frame diagnostic events (maximum 37.6 ms); do not claim identical pacing or no stutter. No resize/occlusion events were logged in either sample.

The first baseline (baseline-r2) averaged 10.431 W but included a 2082 ms event and a screenshot; excluded from the principal comparison. The warm repeat averaged 7.546 W, showing substantial between-run variability. Ordering was initial baseline -> Eco -> warm baseline, not randomized. A single short stationary scene supports a preliminary result, not a battery-runtime estimate or a guarantee for demanding scenes/60 FPS. Background processes, brightness and temperature were not independently controlled or measured. No thermal improvement is claimed.

Evidence in [measurement data](../measurements/2026-10-24-eco/): summaries, all 60 battery samples per arm, and metrics.log excerpts in original order. Logs retain timing, presentation, load, keep-alive, autoload and window events; verbose renderer logs and profile/save files are excluded. Byte offsets in original summaries refer to the full local logs, not these excerpts. The original local runner is not included because it depends on machine-specific app/profile paths. The first failed launch lacked the shader-service dependency and produced no measurement.

## Collection caveats

The inherited Tail reader concatenates its head and tail, duplicating short sampling segments. Summary load_windows, spin_windows and long_frame_count can therefore count the same observation twice; they are not independent sample counts. Reported means are unchanged when the segment is duplicated uniformly. Use metrics.log for unique chronological log events and samples.csv for the 60 actual battery readings. The clean flag is the runner's heuristic, not a guarantee of zero frame overruns. The final 300-frame timing window and long-frame diagnostic maximum cover different data and should not be equated.

## Reproduction protocol

Build build/totk-native-v121 from this commit with matching generated game headers and the native shader-service dependency. Run against the installed 1.2.1 rewritten modules and RomFS, with isolated copies of one save profile and one shared shader cache. Set TOTK_FPS60=0, TOTK_AUTOLOAD=1, TOTK_REALTIME=1, TOTK_WINDOW_SIZE=1280x720 and the app's GPU submission defaults (ALLOW_SUBMIT/WIDEN/BLOCKRT/MIDCLEAR=1, BUDGET_MB=5000). Keep the Mac on battery, wait 60 seconds after the autoload-start marker, and sample AppleSmartBattery every 2 seconds for 120 seconds. For Eco, append `key 22 100` to the isolated TOTK_INPUT_FILE after settle, verify the Eco log marker, then wait another 15 seconds before collection (the original runner also waited 5 seconds before toggling). Do not resize, occlude, move the character or capture screenshots during sampling. Repeat both modes in alternating order for stronger causal confidence.
